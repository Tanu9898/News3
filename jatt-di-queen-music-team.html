Jatt Di Queen Music Team: Jay K, Goldboy, Amber Vashisht and Kerryy Join Upcoming Punjabi Film

The upcoming Punjabi movie Jatt Di Queen is bringing together a notable music and songwriting team as the film prepares for its theatrical release in 2027. Presented by Starmoon Film Productions and Chakora Productions, the movie is scheduled to release in cinemas on 12 February 2027.

The first-look poster of the film has already been unveiled, with the makers receiving an encouraging response from audiences on social media.

Jatt Di Queen Features a Strong Music Lineup

Music is expected to play an important role in Jatt Di Queen, with Jay K, Goldboy, Amber Vashisht and Kerryy credited as Music Directors.

The film also features a songwriting team comprising Happy Raikoti, Youngveer, Amber Vashisht, Abeer and Sufraaz.

With several recognised names involved in the music department, the soundtrack could become an important part of the film's promotional journey as its release approaches.

Manoj Gadarpuriya and Sachin Dahar Among Singers

The singing lineup associated with Jatt Di Queen includes Manoj Gadarpuriya and Sachin Dahar, while Malwai Partap is credited as a Playback Singer.

The involvement of multiple singers and lyricists adds to the musical profile of the upcoming Punjabi film.

Jatt Di Queen Cast and Crew

The film stars King B Chouhan, Rumman Ahmad, Malwai Partap, Sammy Gill, Honey Mattu, Neha Dayal, Pahulpreet Singh, Lucky Kalsi, Deush Mehra and Sukhdev Barnala.

Jatt Di Queen is directed by Arun Kumar Barnwal and written by Sumit Bhatt. The producers are Prabh Goraya and Vicky Kamboj, while Ajay Maurya serves as Executive Producer.

Cinematography is handled by Harnek Singh and Rah Sharma, with Rohit Dhiman as Editor.

Creative Team Behind the Songs

The title song's editing is credited to Gurjeet Singh, while Monika Rani serves as Title Song Choreographer. Deush Mehra is credited as Choreographer.

The technical team also includes Jasdip Singh as Assistant Director, Harshdeep Singh as Art Director, Vishal as Camera Operator, Jaskaran Chogawan as DIT, Sharandeep Singh as Gaffer Assistant, Manpreet Singh as Focus Puller and Kuldeep Singh as Gaffer.

Jatt Di Queen Release Date

Post-production is being handled by Rabab Studio, with AME Digital as the Digital Partner. Poster stills are credited to K Raj Famous Films, while Manoj Zore has designed the poster.

The first-look poster marks the beginning of the promotional campaign for the movie.

Jatt Di Queen will release in cinemas on 12 February 2027, giving Punjabi cinema audiences a new theatrical release to look forward to around Valentine's Day.
