Stephen Fleming Named As New Test Coach

Fleming is set to commence his new position following the recent management restructuring, promising a new era for the national team's test format strategy. The board has cited his incredible track record and calm demeanor as the primary reasons for his appointment.

During his press conference today, Fleming emphasized the need for patience and a strong focus on grassroots development to secure the long-term future of the test squad.
