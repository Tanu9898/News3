Kamz Inkzone Launches Inkboy Musik in 2026 to Build a Multi-Genre Music and Production Catalogue

The Punjabi music industry continues to see artists expand beyond traditional roles, and Kamaljit Singh, professionally known as Kamz Inkzone, is taking a similar route with the launch of his own music and production company, Inkboy Musik.

Established in 2026, Inkboy Musik represents a new chapter in Kamz Inkzone's career after years of experience as an artist, music creator, songwriter, DJ and production professional.

What Is Inkboy Musik?

Inkboy Musik is Kamz Inkzone's own music video production and music company, created to work with artists across multiple genres.

The company's catalogue includes music spanning Qawwali, hip-hop, DJ remixes and commercial Punjabi songs.

The objective is to create a platform where different types of artists can develop and release music while bringing together production, music and visual content.

Kamz Inkzone's Experience Behind Inkboy Musik

Before establishing Inkboy Musik, Kamz Inkzone had already spent years working in the music industry.

His journey began in 2017 with his first track, “Ink You.” He later gained experience working with Speed Records, where he learned about editing, music, lyrics, videos and different stages of music production.

This experience helped him develop the ability to work independently on songs and music videos.

From Artist to Music Producer

Kamz Inkzone's work gradually expanded from performing to creating music for other artists.

After successful projects, more singers began approaching him for music production and new music projects. He also became involved in launching emerging artists.

One notable example is Hunar Sidhu's “Sade Siro,” which went on to cross 25 million views.

Collaboration With Ninja and Bohemia

Kamz Inkzone's career includes collaborations with established names in the Punjabi music industry.

In 2019, he worked with Ninja on the rap track “Befikra.” He later featured with Bohemia, adding another major collaboration to his career.

These projects became part of his wider journey across Punjabi music and rap.

Exploring New Sounds

Kamz Inkzone has continued to explore different styles rather than restricting himself to one genre.

After entering DJ music, he began creating DJ songs. From 2025, he also started working on Afro music remixes and Punjabi remixes, while producing original music featuring other artists.

His interest in Punjabi literature, including the works of Shiv Kumar Batalvi and Bulleh Shah, has also influenced his approach to songwriting.

International Music Projects

Kamz Inkzone's work has also taken him to London, Dubai and Europe, where he has participated in shows, private performances and music video projects.

The international exposure has added another dimension to his career and creative experience.

Inkboy Musik's Multi-Genre Catalogue

With Inkboy Musik, Kamz Inkzone aims to bring together artists working in different areas of music.

The catalogue includes:

  • Qawwali
  • Hip-hop
  • DJ remixes
  • Commercial Punjabi music
  • Original music
  • Featured artist collaborations

The launch of Inkboy Musik marks Kamz Inkzone's transition into another phase of his career—one where he is building not only his own identity as an artist but also a platform for other musicians and creators.

As the company develops, Inkboy Musik is set to become an important part of Kamz Inkzone's continuing journey in music production and entertainment.
