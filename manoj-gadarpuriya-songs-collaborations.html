Manoj Gadarpuriya Is Building His Music Catalogue Through Strong Collaborations

The modern independent music industry has changed the way artists build their audiences. A singer is no longer defined only by solo releases. Collaborations, digital platforms and a consistent catalogue can all play an important role in creating a recognizable musical identity.

Manoj Gadarpuriya is taking that approach with a growing collection of songs that brings him together with different artists and explores a variety of musical directions.

From working with Malwai Partap on energetic projects to collaborating with Harry Balachour on more melodic releases, Manoj Gadarpuriya's recent work shows an artist who is using collaboration as an important part of his musical journey.

A Catalogue Built With Different Artists

One of the noticeable aspects of Manoj Gadarpuriya's recent music is the number of collaborative projects connected to his name.

His catalogue includes “My State My Pride” and “KUDI FIRE” with Malwai Partap, while “Adat Ban Gai”features Harry Balachour. He has also appeared alongside Malwai Partap on “Built Different.”

These collaborations give each project its own identity while allowing Manoj Gadarpuriya to work across different musical ideas.

Working With Malwai Partap

The creative connection between Manoj Gadarpuriya and Malwai Partap has produced several recent projects.

Their release “My State My Pride” was released in July 2026 and is credited to Manoj Gadarpuriya featuring Malwai Partap. The track is part of Manoj Gadarpuriya's recent run of releases.

The two artists also came together for “KUDI FIRE,” a 3-minute, 8-second track credited to Manoj Gadarpuriya featuring Malwai Partap.

Their collaboration continued with “Built Different,” released in August 2026, where Manoj Gadarpuriya is featured on a Malwai Partap release.

The repeated collaboration suggests that the two artists have developed a creative connection that extends across multiple projects.

Harry Balachour Brings Another Dimension

Manoj Gadarpuriya's collaboration with Harry Balachour takes his music in another direction.

Their track “Adat Ban Gai” is credited to Manoj Gadarpuriya featuring Harry Balachour and has a runtime of four minutes.

The pairing also appears on the newer romantic project “Teri Khushbo,” where Harry Balachour is credited for music and featuring.

This gives the collaboration a different character from Manoj Gadarpuriya's projects with Malwai Partap.

Why Collaborations Matter

For independent artists, collaborations can introduce music to completely new audiences.

When two artists work together, listeners who already follow one artist may discover the other for the first time. This makes collaborations particularly useful for artists developing their presence in the digital music space.

For Manoj Gadarpuriya, working with different artists also allows him to experiment with different sounds instead of limiting his catalogue to a single style.

“My City” Shows His Solo Side

While collaborations are an important part of his current catalogue, Manoj Gadarpuriya is also developing his identity through solo music.

“My City” was released on July 7, 2026 as a Manoj Gadarpuriya single. The track has a runtime of 2 minutes and 11 seconds and is credited under Manoj Gadarpuriya Music.

The release is important because it gives listeners a project that is centered entirely on Manoj Gadarpuriya rather than being built around another featured artist.

Together, his solo and collaborative releases are creating a broader picture of his musical identity.

More Than One Collaboration, More Than One Sound

Looking at Manoj Gadarpuriya's recent catalogue, it becomes clear that collaboration is not being used for just one type of song.

There are projects with Malwai Partap, projects with Harry Balachour, and solo releases such as “My City.”

Each project adds something different to his catalogue.

That variety is helping Manoj Gadarpuriya establish himself through a body of work rather than relying on a single release to define his name.

The Collaboration Journey Continues

With several recent collaborations already part of his catalogue, Manoj Gadarpuriya's future projects will be interesting to follow.

His work with Malwai Partap demonstrates his ability to participate in energetic collaborative releases, while his work with Harry Balachour shows another side of his musical direction.

As more songs are released, these collaborations can become an important part of the story surrounding Manoj Gadarpuriya music.

Conclusion

Manoj Gadarpuriya's recent music demonstrates how collaboration can become an important tool for an independent artist.

From “My State My Pride,” “KUDI FIRE,” and “Built Different” with Malwai Partap to “Adat Ban Gai” and “Teri Khushbo” with Harry Balachour, his catalogue continues to expand through different creative partnerships.

Combined with solo releases such as “My City,” these projects show an artist building his musical identity through consistency, experimentation and collaboration.

For Manoj Gadarpuriya, every collaboration is becoming another opportunity to explore a new sound and reach another audience.
